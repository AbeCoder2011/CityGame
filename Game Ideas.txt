- basics
Citygame met progressieve unlocks van gebouwen
Steeds betere samenwerkingen van gebouwen
5 Difficulties die steeds moeilijker en grindy worden.

- Features
Procedurally generated terrain with forests, mountains and lakes/rivers
Unlock more area for your city by completing achievements
Have many different categories combine to make the best money.
Have global buildings that give useful buffs, unlocked through the town hall.
Many categories like Working, Entertainment, Power, Nature, and other interesting buildings
Trains to connect parts of your city and transport goods
